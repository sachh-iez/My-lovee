Hi Hal, happy 1st anniversary, my love. 💜

I just want to say that I love you so much, more than I can probably explain through words. Thank you for staying with me, especially during the times na hindi naging okay ang lahat. I know na we've been in bad situations sometimes, and there were moments when things became difficult for both of us. We’ve had misunderstandings, challenges, and days when things weren't as easy as we wanted them to be. But despite all of that, andito ka pa rin. You stayed, you listened, you understood me, and you continued choosing to be with me.

And honestly, that means so much to me.

Thank you for every day that you choose me. Thank you for choosing us even when things aren't perfect. Thank you for giving us chances to grow, to understand each other better, and to become better for each other. I appreciate every little thing you do, even the things you might think are small or ordinary. To me, those little things are part of what makes our relationship special.

Thank you for all the memories we've made during these 12 months. The happy moments, the silly moments, the random conversations, the times we laughed over something that wasn't even that funny, and even the difficult moments that taught us something about each other. Every moment became a part of our story, and I'm grateful that I got to experience them with you.

I know one year might still be just the beginning, and I know we’ll probably face more challenges along the way. But I hope that whenever things get difficult, we remember why we chose each other in the first place. I hope we continue to communicate, understand each other, support each other, and choose each other even on the days when things aren't easy.

I’m really thankful that out of all the people I could have met, I met you. You became someone very special to me, someone who became a big part of my everyday life and my memories. And I hope you know how much I appreciate having you in my life.

I love you very much, my love. Thank you for staying. Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for choosing us.

Happy 1st anniversary, Hal. 💜

Here's to our first 12 months, all the memories we've made, everything we've learned together, and hopefully many more moments that we can look back on someday and smile about.

I love you always. Happy anniversary, my love. 🌻🐱💜

🎵 Songs I Dedicated to You

1. “Miss Independent” — Ne-Yo
I chose this song because I admire how strong and independent you are. You have your own personality, your own way of doing things, and your own dreams, and that's one of the things I appreciate about you. I don't want you to ever feel like you have to change who you are just to be loved. I love you for being you, and I’m proud of the person you are.

2. “Libu-Libong Buwan (Uuwian)” — Kyle Raphael
I dedicated this one because it reminds me of the feeling of having someone you want to come back to. Through all the days, months, and everything life brings, there’s something comforting about knowing there’s someone who feels like home. After everything we've been through, I hope we can continue being that kind of safe place for each other.

3. “Paninindigan” — Ben&Ben
This song reminds me of choosing someone and standing by that choice. For me, it connects to the part of our relationship where we've faced challenges but still decided to stay and work things out. Love isn't always about the easy days; sometimes it's about choosing to understand, choosing to communicate, and choosing to stay committed to what you have together.

4. “Tahanan” — El Manu
I chose this song because of the feeling behind the word “tahanan” — a place where you feel comfortable, safe, and accepted. In our relationship, I hope you always know that you can be yourself with me. I want our relationship to be a place where we can be honest about our feelings, support each other, and feel like we don't have to face everything alone.

5. “Aya” — Earl Agustin
I chose “Aya” because it reminds me of the simple, quiet feeling of caring deeply for someone. It makes me think of the little moments we share—the conversations, the memories, and just having someone special to think about. Sometimes you don't need a big reason to love someone. You just do, and I'm happy that person is you. 💜
